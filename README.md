Meetergo provides an all‑in‑one business platform offering appointment booking, video meetings, CRM, digital signatures, invoicing and more. The SaaS solution is GDPR‑compliant, hosted on EU servers, and integrates 18 business apps to help companies manage scheduling, customer relations and payments from a single interface.

**Areas:** SaaS · Scheduling · CRM · VideoMeetings · Invoicing

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for meetergo: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
